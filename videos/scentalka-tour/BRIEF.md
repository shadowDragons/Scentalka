## Intent

An animated walkthrough of the Scentalka English-learning life simulator. Show how a learner sets up a personal world, meets recurring people, handles ordinary situations, and chooses what to say or do. The tone is clear, calm, and product-led. The English UI is part of the experience.

## Assets

- `../../原型/Scentalka Prototype.html` — source application and visual reference.
- `../../原型/verification/` — verified screenshots of representative world, hospital, road, task, timeline, and voice-review states.

## Customizations

- Cover the eight previously reviewed storyboard beats, including first-time setup, world choices, NPCs, daily scenarios, planning, personal records, and voice-message approval.
- Use concise English on-screen text, with no voiceover, music, or sound effects.

## Notes

- Treat the working prototype as the visual source of truth. Use captured prototype screens as the base; do not invent product behavior or present animation-only scenes as live application states.
